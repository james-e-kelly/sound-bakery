10 * Build configuration guards, shared by Sound Chef (C) and Sound Bakery (C++).
11 *
12 * Normally defined by CMake via the SoundBakery::BuildConfig target
13 * (cmake/setup_build_config.cmake), which maps them Wwise/FMOD-style:
14 *
15 * SBK_CONFIG_DEBUG - full checks, everything on (CMake Debug)
16 * SBK_CONFIG_PROFILE - optimized but instrumented (CMake RelWithDebInfo)
17 * SBK_CONFIG_RELEASE - optimized, tooling compiled out (CMake Release/MinSizeRel)
18 * SBK_CONFIG_ENABLE_LOGGING - logging compiled in (default: not Release)
19 * SBK_CONFIG_ENABLE_PROFILING- profiling/remote connections compiled in (default: not Release)
20 *
21 * Always defined to 0 or 1 - test with `#if`, never `#ifdef`. The fallbacks
22 * below only apply when building against the headers without the CMake
23 * target, and derive a sensible mapping from NDEBUG.
24 */
