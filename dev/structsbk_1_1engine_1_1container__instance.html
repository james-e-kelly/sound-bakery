Detailed Description

A runtime, playing representation of a sbk::engine::container.

If the container is playing a sound, it has a voice handle. If the container is playing child sounds, it has a child count.

All nodes know their parent index (index into the voice's container array). Once an instance ends, it can tell its parent that it's finished, decrementing its child count.

Instances are finished when their sc_voice_handle has ended or its child count is 0.
